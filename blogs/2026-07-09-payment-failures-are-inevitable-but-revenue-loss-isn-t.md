Failed payments cost the average subscription business around 10% of ARR every year. The cause isn’t customers choosing to cancel, it’s payment failures that end relationships they never intended to end. And it’s easy to see where subscribers slip away: dunning emails go unread, the portal has one too many steps to update a card.
